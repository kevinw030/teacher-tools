Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

The Short Version

  • You need a free account to use the tools and games. Students who join a class activity with a class code never need an account.
  • If you make an account, we save your first name, email and the things you make, so you can use them on any computer.
  • People who join an activity with a class code don't make an account, and nothing about them is saved.
  • We count which pages are opened so we can make the site better, without saving your IP address or anything you type.
  • Student names a teacher types in are only ever shown back to that teacher. We never sell them, use them for ads, or use them to train AI.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it yourself, any time, from My Account.
  • We never sell your information or show ads.

Who We Are

ED Sidekick (edsidekick.com) is a set of classroom tools and games for teachers, parents and anyone who leads a group. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean ED Sidekick, and "you" means the person using the site.

Before You Sign In

Anyone can look at the home page, the list of games, this page and the Terms of Service without an account. The tools and games themselves need a free account. Students who join a class activity at edsidekick.com/join never need one.

Who Can Make an Account

Anyone who is 13 or older can make an account. Accounts are not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

What We Save When You Have an Account

  • Your first name and email address. We use these to sign you in, greet you on the site, and send account emails such as password resets.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. It is stored in scrambled (hashed) form. We can't see it.
  • The things you make and save: classroom boards, class lists, your own questions, schedules, class jobs, seating charts, exit tickets and settings.
  • Your plan (Free, Plus or Premium) so the right features are turned on.

This information is stored with our database and sign-in provider, Supabase. Only you can see your saved work when you are signed in.

Student Data

ED Sidekick is built so that students never have to give us their information. Here is exactly how student information is handled.

What we hold

  • Only what a teacher types in. Tools like the Name Picker, Group Maker, Class Jobs, Class Points and Seating Chart let a teacher type in a class list. We ask for first names only, and nicknames or initials work just as well.
  • Students never make accounts. Accounts are for people 13 and older, and they are meant for the adult running the class.
  • Class-code games save nothing. When students join a game at edsidekick.com/join, the name they type and their game picks only exist while the game is running. Nothing about them is stored once it ends.

How we use it

  • Class lists are saved to the teacher's account so they show up on any computer the teacher signs in on. Only that signed-in teacher can see them.
  • We use student names for one thing: showing them back to the teacher inside the tools.
  • We never sell student information, never use it for advertising, never build profiles of students, and never use it to train artificial intelligence.
  • Student names are never part of our visit counts.

The teacher and school stay in charge

  • A teacher can change or delete any name at any time, and deleting the account in My Account removes every class list right away.
  • When a teacher uses ED Sidekick with a class, we handle the class list on behalf of the teacher and their school, and only to run the tools. The school keeps control of its students' records, as the U.S. Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) expects.
  • Because children under 13 never make accounts and never give us information directly, ED Sidekick does not collect personal information from children as described in the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Teachers should still follow their school's or district's rules about what student information they type in.
  • If we ever learn that someone got into student information without permission, we will tell the affected teachers promptly.

Schools and districts

If your school or district needs a Student Data Privacy Agreement or has questions about how ED Sidekick handles student information, email hello@edsidekick.com.

People Who Join With a Class Code

Some games and activities can share a class code so others can join in. People who join with a code don't make an account, and nothing about them is saved: no name, birthday, location or anything else. When a class answers an Exit Ticket question with a code, the teacher's account keeps the answers only, with no names attached.

Signing In With Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use them only to create your account and sign you in. We do not get your Google password, and we don't access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

ED Sidekick's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Payments

Paid plans are handled by Stripe, a payment company. Your card details go straight to Stripe and are never stored on ED Sidekick. We receive a record of your plan, the amount paid, and whether a payment went through, so we can turn your plan on and keep our books.

Counting Visits

To learn which tools and games are most useful, ED Sidekick keeps a simple count of pages opened. Each time a page opens we save:

  • which page or tool was opened (for example "Timer"), and the date and time
  • whether it was opened on a phone, tablet or computer
  • the name of the website that sent you here, if any (for example "google.com"), on the first page of a visit
  • a random visitor number your browser makes for itself, so one person opening ten pages counts as one visitor
  • a few in-tool choices, like which escape room and grade was played, or which Plus feature someone clicked
  • your account, if you are signed in

We do not save your IP address, location, or anything you type into a tool, and student names are never part of it. Only the site owner can see these counts. If your browser is set to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted. You can also clear your browser's site data at any time to get a new random visitor number.

Cookies and Browser Storage

We use your browser's storage to save your work on your computer, to keep you signed in, and to hold the random visitor number described above. We don't use advertising cookies or outside tracking tools, and we don't follow you around other websites.

Services We Rely On

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Supabase stores accounts, saved work and the visit counts.
  • Google provides "Continue with Google" sign-in and the fonts used on the site.
  • Stripe handles payments for paid plans.
  • Resend sends account emails, like confirming your email address or resetting your password.

Like most websites, these services may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses and browser type) to run and protect their systems.

What We Never Do

  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • We never show ads or share your information with advertisers.
  • We never use your saved work or class lists for anything except giving them back to you.

Your Choices

  • You can see and change your saved work at any time on the site.
  • You can sign out at any time. Your work stays in your account for next time.
  • You can delete your account and everything saved in it yourself, any time: open My Account and choose Delete My Account. It happens right away. (If you have Plus, cancel it first so you aren't charged again.)
  • You can also email us to see, fix or delete your information, and we will do it within 30 days.
  • Payment records are kept for tax and accounting, but they are no longer linked to you once your account is deleted.

Keeping Information Safe

Accounts are protected by sign-in, the connection to the site is encrypted (HTTPS), and the database only lets each person reach their own saved work. No system is perfect, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is important, we will let account holders know on the site or by email.
