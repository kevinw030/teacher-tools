Getting Started

Is ED Sidekick free?

Yes. The free plan includes every board widget, every Daily tool and every game except Trivia Showdown. You just need a free account to open the tools. Plus adds unlimited saved boards and class lists, all four escape rooms, Trivia Showdown, Seating Chart, Prompts and more. See Plans and Pricing.

Why do I need an account?

An account keeps the tools for the adult running the class, and it saves your boards, class lists and settings so they show up on any computer you sign in on, like your school computer and your laptop at home.

Accounts are for people 13 and older. Students never need one.

How do I sign up?

Go to My Account and choose Continue with Google, or type your first name, email and a password. If you use email, we send you a message to confirm your address. Open it and press Confirm My Email.

What works best on the classroom screen?

Open the Classroom Board on the computer connected to your projector or smart board and go full screen. Add a timer, name picker, sound meter, text and more, then save the screen to use again tomorrow.

Students Joining

How do students join a game?
  1. Start a class game, like Class Bingo. A six-letter class code and a QR code appear on your screen.
  2. Students go to edsidekick.com/join on their own device, or scan the QR code.
  3. They type the code and a first name or nickname, then wait for you to press Start.
Do students need an account or email?

No. Students never make accounts. Nothing about a student who joins with a code is saved: the name they type only exists while the game is running.

A student typed a silly name. Can I remove them?

Yes. In the waiting room, tap a student's name on your screen to remove them. Names also go through a kid-safe word filter.

Saving Your Work

Where is my work saved?

When you're signed in, your boards, class lists, questions, schedules, seating charts and settings save to your account automatically. Sign in on any computer and they're there.

How do I make a backup?

To use another computer, just sign in there. If you also want a copy to keep yourself, open Settings (from your account icon) and choose Save Backup File. Use Load Backup File to bring it back.

How many boards and class lists can I save?

The free plan saves 2 board screens and 2 class lists. Plus saves as many as you like.

Plans and Billing

How much is Plus?

Current prices, including any Early Access discount, are on the Plans and Pricing tab in My Account. You can pay monthly or once a year.

How do I cancel Plus?

Open My Account and choose Manage My Plan. That opens a secure Stripe page where you can cancel. You keep Plus until the end of the month or year you paid for, then your account moves to the free plan. Nothing you made is deleted.

Can I get a refund?

If ED Sidekick isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund. Renewals aren't refunded, so cancel before your renewal date. The full details are in the Terms of Service.

Can I change my card or get a receipt?

Yes. Manage My Plan in My Account lets you update your card and download receipts.

I paid, but my account still says Free.

It usually turns on within a few seconds. Refresh My Account. If it still says Free after a few minutes, check that you're signed in with the same email you paid with, then email us and we'll sort it out.

Can my school pay?

A teacher can pay with a school card through the normal checkout. If your school needs an invoice or a quote, email us.

Privacy and Student Data

Is it safe to type in student names?

We ask for first names only, and nicknames or initials work too. Names you type are saved to your account, and only you can see them. We never sell them, use them for ads, or use them to train AI. Please follow your school's rules about student information. Read more in the Student Data section of our Privacy Policy.

My district needs a data privacy agreement.

Email hello@edsidekick.com with your district's agreement or questions and we'll work through it with you.

How do I delete my account?

Open My Account, scroll down and choose Delete My Account. It removes your account and everything in it right away. If you have Plus, cancel it first so you aren't charged again.

Fixing Problems

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot Password? and type your email. We'll send a link to choose a new one. If you signed up with Google, use Continue with Google instead.

I didn't get the email.

Emails come from hello@edsidekick.com and usually arrive within a minute. Check your spam or junk folder. School email systems sometimes hold outside messages, so try a personal address or ask your tech team to allow edsidekick.com.

The site is blocked at my school.

School web filters sometimes block new websites until they're reviewed. Ask your school's tech team to allow edsidekick.com. It's an education site with no ads.

Something looks broken.

Try refreshing the page first. If it's still wrong, email hello@edsidekick.com with what you were doing and, if you can, a screenshot.

Still stuck? Email hello@edsidekick.com.
